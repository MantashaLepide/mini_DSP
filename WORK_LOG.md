- Cards are maximal 13-19 digit candidates with horizontal spaces/hyphens and valid Luhn, not verified payment accounts. Numeric candidates never cross newlines. A continuous digit/space/hyphen run is one candidate to avoid extracting valid substrings from malformed/overlong values; separate numeric occurrences with commas, semicolons, newlines, or intervening text. Whitespace-only separation of multiple numbers on one line is ambiguous and may not count separately.
